Request from Us

We advocate for people with special needs. Items are available to be requested if you are in need.

Eligibility

To be eligible, you should meet at least one criterion:

  • You are a registered nonprofit organization supporting a special-needs population.
  • You are a parent of a special-needs child and your family income is below $40,000 USD.

How to Request

Submit a Request Form

We do not donate money or cash to individuals or other organizations.
